App stops working after screen timeout.

Your device manufacturer (e.g., Xiaomi, Samsung) aggressively terminates background processes. Lock Vindly in the recent tasks overview (padlock icon) and check dontkillmyapp.com for OEM-specific settings.

The map does not come back after a call or after returning to the home screen.

Do not swipe Vindly away from recent apps. Swiping it out kills background monitoring and halts map restore. Make sure AccessibilityService is enabled.

Is there a free trial for the Premium plan?

Yes. The annual subscription includes a 1-month free trial (30 days) on Google Play (then $29.99/year). A flexible monthly plan is also available without a trial.

Does the free plan restore the map from every app?

No. The free core (Navigation Bridge) covers phone calls and returning to the home screen launcher. Custom return timers for other applications (such as Spotify or messaging apps) are part of the Premium plan (Intelligent Navigation Bridge).
